# THE HORSE THAT WAITS: series bible

An interactive mystery-horror series told in first person by Daniel. Each episode ends with
**A** (the safe choice) or **B** (the deeper choice); viewers vote in the comments and the winning
option becomes the next episode. The story is eerie and quiet, never explicit. The horse is a
supernatural guide, not a romantic or sexual subject.

## The premise

Daniel's partner Karis walks into the tree line behind their house and does not come back. On the
seventh night, a white horse appears outside his window with no rider. It seems to know what he
lost. Daniel follows it into a forest path that was not there before, and finds Karis waiting in a
clearing — with rules she refuses to explain.

## The rules of the world

- The white horse appears only to people who have **lost something**. It never speaks.
- It does not attack. It **leads**. If you follow, the path changes.
- The forest keeps time in **seven-day loops**. If the follower looks away, the days start again.
- The horse stands **between the lost person and the thing that is not seen**. It blocks passage;
  it does not explain.
- **Secret truth (do not reveal early):** whoever follows the horse to the end of the path does not
  come back the same. The horse is not returning people — it is choosing them.
- **Content guard:** no sexual content involving the horse or any character. All contact with the
  horse is non-verbal, mysterious, and safe.

## Canon facts fixed by released episodes

- The horse appears outside Daniel's window on the **seventh night** after Karis disappears.
- Karis walked past the **last streetlight** and into the trees seven days earlier.
- Daniel searched every morning and called every evening. On day six he taped Karis's face to a
  pole; by morning the tape was cut clean.
- The horse is **white**, has **no rider**, and does not neigh — it breathes like someone standing
  in the cold.
- The horse turns its head toward the tree line; Daniel follows it past the streetlight, past a
  warning sign, onto a path that was not there before.
- Karis is sitting in a clearing, unharmed, with her hands in her lap. She tells Daniel not to come
  closer.
- Karis says the horse only shows itself to people who have lost something.
- The horse strikes one hoof **once, then twice**. Karis says: "Those are the days. Seven. If you
  look away, the forest starts them again."
- A **third footstep** lands in the leaves behind Karis. Nothing is there. The horse steps between
  Daniel and the space, and will not let him pass.
- Daniel understands that the horse did not bring him to Karis; it brought Karis to the choice.
