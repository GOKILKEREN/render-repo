# TemenHilang3D storyboard — POV: My Friend Vanished for 7 Days

Format: 1080x1920, 30 fps, 131 seconds (3930 frames). Scene lengths follow the natural VO takes: 17 / 16 / 15 / 15 / 15 / 17 / 18 / 18 s. Voice-over: Bahasa Indonesia gaul (ElevenLabs `eleven_multilingual_v2`, speed 1.0). Every scene also carries a short English caption line for viewers outside Indonesia. Captions are short readouts, never a word-for-word transcript of the narration.

Safe area: top 150, right 200, bottom 250, left 60. All text lives inside that box.

Style: cartoon-bold 3D, rounded original characters, dark cinematic pools of light, cyan / violet / amber accents, visible emitters only. No real brands, no famous-character silhouettes, no stick figures.

## Retention spine

- **Hook (frame 0):** `POV: TEMEN KAMU HILANG 7 HARI` is on screen immediately, with the glowing-phone bedroom already up (no fade from black). Open loop: *where did Bimo go?*
- **Body:** a daily journal — Day 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 — each beat a bigger, more absurd dead end. Uneven visual interrupts every ~2-4 s inside each scene (camera push, flash, ring pulse, poster pop).
- **Payoff (scene 8):** the loop closes with the clop-clop horse reveal and the deadpan "why no text?!" double take.

## Sound logic

Voice leads. One soft accent per scene change (phone ping, ring pulse, stamp, hoof tap, paper flutter, photo shimmer, clop, final clop + sting). No music under the words in the speech band; the CI loudnorm pass masters to -14 LUFS. The single deliberate silence is the half-beat before the scene-8 reveal.

## Twist construction (anticipation -> payoff)

1. Scene 4 plants the hoofprints and the narrator dismisses them ("am I crazy?").
2. Scene 6 plants the horse photo and the narrator still does not connect it.
3. Scene 7 winds up with sound-before-sight: clops in the dark, a growing shadow, a held breath.
4. Scene 8 pays off with the literal horse — a subversion, because the "missing person" was never in danger, just stuck on a rented horse with no phone. The deadpan double line closes the loop.
