# What if iron disappears?

Format: YouTube Shorts, 1080x1920 vertical, 30 fps, 150 seconds target.
Audience: Global and international, English script and English voiceover.
Master target: -14 LUFS integrated, true peak at or below -1 dBTP.

## Style Spine

- aesthetic: Hand drawn paper cut explainer with charcoal outlines, warm paper texture, and sudden cold blue voids where iron vanishes.
- palette_anchors: cream paper #F6ECD8, charcoal #2B2622, rust red #C94E32, steel blue #5C7C99, ash gray #8E8A82.
- lighting_logic: Soft overhead storybook light on paper textures, hard white flash at each iron loss event, low amber light for the primitive ending.
- camera_grammar: Flat 2D staging with push ins, drop reveals, and quick vertical wipes between days.
- lens_character: No real lens, exaggerated depth by scale, foreground props larger than background ruins.
- motion_tempo: 1.2 to 1.8 second average shot length, faster in Day 1, slower in Day 365.
- audio_world: Tight narration, sparse mechanical impacts, low room tone, sparse music bed that leaves 18 dB under speech.
- audio_palette: Tick for disappearing atoms, thud for collapse, whoosh for time jumps, click for tools failing, chime for the final reveal.
- scene_transitions: white flash into vertical paper wipe, each day marked by one distinct impact sound.
- forbidden_visual: No real gore, no famous cartoon characters, no text outside the safe rectangle, no small dense labels.
- forbidden_audio: No lyric music, no effect on every cut, no loud bed under narration.

## Fact anchors

- Iron in hemoglobin is required for oxygen binding and transport.
- Steel is an iron alloy, so removing iron removes most steel.
- Iron for Earth born metals is produced by stellar nucleosynthesis and supernova events.
- Food production and modern surgery rely heavily on iron and steel tools.

## Audio plan summary

- Voice leads throughout. Music bed is generated in project, low, and ducked under speech.
- Day transitions use one accent each, never a sound on every cut.
- First sound starts on frame 0. No digital silence longer than 0.6 seconds.
